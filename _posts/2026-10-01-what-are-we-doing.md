We've been releasing music for 22 years as of 2026. Garish Dress is a home for us to collect our output and have ownership in perpetuity and a channel for communication that's malleable to however we want to communicate at any given time.  

As of the date of this entry, the website is "soft launched". There's kinks to be worked out, and some features I hope to add later. But if you're reading this close to the date I published it, thanks for being curious enough to poke around.
